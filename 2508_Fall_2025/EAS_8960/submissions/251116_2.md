### Summary of Talk (minimum 150 words).
Tailoring the document to match the specific job qualifications is the most crucial aspect for writing an effective resume. One must aim for a clean, consistent, and readable format using straight lines, standard margins, and a professional font size of 10.5 to 12 points. Most of all, one should avoid using templates to prevent formatting obstacles and subjective "design gimmicks."

The content of the resume should follow a strategic reverse chronological order using custom headings. To write strong descriptions, follow the action formula: start every point with a strong action verb, be specific, and quantify your results. Give the most space to the most relevant experience, showcasing the application of skills, not just duties.

One may categorize the technical skills using subheadings to showcase the depth. Using qualifying language like beginner or intermediate for new skills are helpful too. Finally, to avoid age discrimination, only include the last 10 years of experience and omit graduation dates. Conservative formatting is recommended, as elaborate design often struggles with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
