### Summary of Talk (minimum 150 words).
Emily Parry pointed out that offer negotiation requires thorough preparation and recognizing the leverage a job candidate holds once an offer is extended. Thus, the candidate must establish his or her market value through thorough salary research using sites like Glassdoor and university data, and clearly define your lowest acceptable (walk-away) number.

Then, the candidate should focus on the negotiation on the entire offer (salary, stock, bonus, etc.), not just base pay. His or her position of strength must be built on objective evidence, including the advanced degree, technical skills, and relevant past experience. It is not recommended to use personal financial needs as justification. When negotiating, using confident yet gracious language is important, anchoring the counter-offer to data already researched.

Also, one must be prepared for hard questions like "Do you have other offers?" without ever lying. If facing an exploding offer (a short deadline), immediately ask for more time to consider or disclose competing offers to accelerate the timeline.
